Paper cup sizes: which size suits your café?

Art Zone Printing prepress team·Updated: 25 October 2026

Paper cups are usually sized in fluid ounces (oz); one ounce is about 30 ml. Four sizes cover most cafés: 4 oz for espresso, 8 oz for cappuccino and flat white, 12 oz for lattes and regular drinks, and 16 oz for large drinks. Most cafés need only two or three of them.

Common sizes and what goes in them

A cup size refers to its capacity filled roughly to the brim, so the drink you serve is a little less than the stated size. Actual capacity also varies slightly between manufacturers, so treat these figures as approximate.

SizeApprox. capacityTypical use
4 ozabout 120 mlEspresso, Saudi coffee, tasting samples
8 ozabout 240 mlCappuccino, flat white, tea
12 ozabout 355 mlLatte, americano, the regular hot drink
16 ozabout 475 mlLarge lattes and mochas

Cold drinks usually go into larger clear plastic cups with a flat or dome lid, because ice takes up part of the volume.

Single wall or double wall?

  • Single wall: one layer of paper, lower cost, usually needs a sleeve for hot drinks.
  • Double wall: two layers with an insulating gap, held without a sleeve, sturdier and more premium in the hand.
  • Clear plastic: for cold drinks; shows off the drink and usually carries a one- or two-colour logo.

How to choose sizes for your menu

  • Start from the menu: pick the size of your best-selling drink first.
  • Keep the range small: two or three sizes are usually enough and lower printing and storage costs.
  • Share lids where you can: some neighbouring sizes use the same rim diameter, so ask before you order.
  • Think about delivery: double-wall cups keep drinks hot and protect the customer’s hand in transit.

Preparing cup artwork for print

A cup is not printed in 3D: its paper is printed flat as a curved fan shape and then formed. Each size needs its own template (die-line), and the artwork must allow for the seam where the two ends of the paper overlap — keep logos and key text away from it.

  • Ask the printer for the template of your size before you start designing.
  • Keep the logo and text away from the seam and the top and bottom edges.
  • Send the logo as a vector file (PDF, AI or EPS) with your brand colour codes.
  • Approve the digital proof before production starts.

Quick answers

How many ml is an 8 oz cup?

One fluid ounce is about 29.6 ml, so an 8 oz cup holds about 240 ml to the brim and is usually served a little below that.

Which size is used for Saudi coffee?

Saudi coffee is served in small amounts, so small cups of 4 oz or less are typically used.
