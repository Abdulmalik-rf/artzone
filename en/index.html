The production line

From file to freight in five stations.

AZ-LINE / 05
  1. S1

    Prepress

    Files checked, colors profiled, and a digital proof sent for your approval before a single sheet runs.

  2. S2

    Setup

    Presses calibrated, substrates loaded, and color matched to your brand standard.

  3. S3

    Press run

    Your job runs on the press best suited to it — offset for volume, digital for speed, wide format for scale.

  4. S4

    Finishing

    We finish your job to the exact spec — adding a protective coat, shaping it as required, then cutting it to its final form.

  5. S5

    QC & dispatch

    Every batch inspected by hand, packed and shipped to any city in Saudi Arabia.

✱Production starts the day your proof is approved.
