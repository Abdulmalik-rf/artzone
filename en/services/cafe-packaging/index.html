Café and restaurant packaging in Dammam

Art Zone Printing makes everything a café or restaurant needs to print and pack in one order at its plant in Dammam: paper and plastic cups, food, dessert and coffee boxes, paper bags and logo stickers. Everything is produced on the same floor, so it arrives colour-matched. You approve a digital proof before anything is printed, and we deliver to every city in Saudi Arabia.

Questions about this service

01What is in the café packaging bundle?

Paper and plastic cups, food, dessert and coffee boxes, paper bags and logo stickers, with a shop sign as an option. You choose only the items you need.

02Can I order cups, boxes and bags in one order?

Yes. Cups, boxes and bags are produced on the same floor, so they arrive colour-matched in one order, after you approve a digital proof of each item.

03What is the minimum order?

It depends on each item, its size and print method. Send the list of items and rough quantities on WhatsApp and we will reply with the minimum for each item and a quote during working hours.

04How early should I order before my café opens?

Lead time depends on the items, quantities and finishes, and production starts as soon as you approve the proof. Contact us early and include your opening date; we confirm the delivery date with the quote.

05Can you prepare the designs from my café logo?

Yes. Our prepress team prepares the print file for every item from your logo and brand, and sends a digital proof for approval before production.

06Are the inks and materials suitable for food and drinks?

Yes. Cups are printed with food-safe inks approved for food packaging, and food boxes use materials made for food packaging.

07Do you deliver outside Dammam?

Yes, we ship to every city in Saudi Arabia, with tracking on every shipment.
