Questions about this service

01What is the difference between single-wall and double-wall cups?

A single-wall cup has one paper wall and is usually served with a sleeve for hot drinks. A double-wall cup has two walls that insulate the heat, so it can be held without a sleeve and feels more premium in the hand.

02Are the inks safe for drinks?

Yes. Cups are printed with food-safe inks approved for food packaging.

03What is the minimum order?

It depends on the cup type, size and print method. Send us the type, size and rough quantity on WhatsApp and we will reply with the minimum and a quote during working hours.

04Can you design the cup?

Yes. Our prepress team prepares the print file from your logo or idea and sends a digital proof for approval before production.

05Do you deliver outside Dammam?

Yes, we ship to every city in Saudi Arabia, with tracking on every shipment.
